# Delta India demo desk

The current desk uses only the pinned India **testnet** REST and WebSocket endpoints. Public closed 5-minute candles drive the selected catalog candidate. Forming candles and stream marks are for display only. With no credentials you can observe public markets; missing account or market data is not replaced with simulated values.

**Execution starts disabled on every boot.** The server must have `DESK_EXECUTION=delta`, `DELTA_ENV=testnet` and the complete `DELTA_DEMO_API_KEY` / `DELTA_DEMO_API_SECRET` pair. Generic and production credentials are ignored. Select a catalog ID and separately arm autonomous demo scanning. It permits only one position at a time and waits for Delta to report a flat account with no working orders before a subsequent entry. Pause, changing the candidate, any risk/data error or restart disarms it. Use a dedicated testnet account and a single local desk process; unrelated clients can change exchange state after preflight. There is no live unlock, discretionary test-order route, paper ledger, cancel-all or local stop management.

Before submitting, the desk requires a current healthy exchange connection, fresh wallet, a completely flat account **including working orders**, current closed consecutive candles, valid product metadata, a fresh two-sided quote, and allowed drawdown, cooldown, session, news and spread. It sizes whole contracts to the configured risk budget and at most 95% of available USD as notional (no leverage increase). A price-bounded IOC limit carries a mark-triggered bracket: 1% stop, 2% target from executable limit, rounded to the exchange grid. Estimated stop loss includes the larger of 5.9 bps or actual taker commission including 18% GST, plus 2 bps slippage per side and the observed spread. Gaps, fees and slippage may exceed estimates; an entry may not fill. The first valid equity observation per UTC day is persisted as the drawdown baseline, **not** claimed as exchange midnight equity. Legacy sweep/analyst settings do not choose entries.

The signal is claimed in SQLite before submission. A fully confirmed fill leaves the scanner armed, but no further entry is eligible until Delta reports a fresh, flat account with no working orders and the per-symbol cooldown expires. An ambiguous timeout, nonfinal/partial fill, rejection or process crash disarms and can leave `unknown`/`pending`: further entries are refused, including after restart. Do not retry blindly. Reconcile order, fills, positions and brackets in Delta Demo; there is deliberately no API to clear the lock. Use the exchange to manage/close positions, including after a kill switch. Exchange fills are not grouped into estimated closed trades or win rates.

On first open, mixed-provenance SQLite tables (including reviews, orders and settings) are renamed transactionally to `legacy_unverified_*`; none are deleted or imported into active account performance. Keep the same database for the same demo account. Backtest results are separate simulations.
